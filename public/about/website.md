## The Beginning
This website was made impulsive create back in 2023 during my last year of junior High. It was inspired by [Literalhat](https://literalhat.com/) because of course it was. Had no prior knowledge about the indieweb culture and simply found [Neocities](https://neocities.org/) by googling "free hosting". Either way, I've learned a lot about the internet since I began working on this website and am forever indebted to those two things for helping find my passion for programming.

## Algorithms
> I don't like having my interests be dictated by algorithms. I want to be more deliberate about how I spend my time on the internet and what to leave behind. 

To elaborate further on my quote, I don't think algorithms are inherently bad. In fact, I spend quite some time studying them myself! Algorithms are just apathetic rulers that values numbers over social impact. It curates narrow views that too many people passively adopt regardless of it's accuracy. There are too many beautiful things in this world obfuscated by algorithms. Which is why this website exists! It is my way of actively seeking and promoting those beautiful things.

## Design Philosophy
I don't like wasting other people's time. If you come to my website to see my art, that is the first damn thing you're going to see. Which is also why I choose to have a "minimalist" design; Minimalist being relative to other Indieweb designs. Some people claim that using the same layout is *boring* and ***uncreative*** but I personally do not see it that way. I don't want to find myself endlessly writing the same thing for different layouts. I want to spend less time modifying the page for me to put my art in and spend more time actually making the art to put into the page. If you're curious of how everything is put together, check out the [source code](https://github.com/Stupiedidiot/stupied.net)!
