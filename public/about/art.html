I have making art since forever. Honestly cannot recall a time I was never not drawing and writing stories. Art has always played an important role in my life. It has help me compartmentalized my experiences. Genuinely don't know what I'd be doing without it.

My Philosophy

Something is better than nothing

Generally, people idolize the tortured artist who spend their waking moment perfecting that one thing. Unfortunately, my career path isn't related to art and committing such a role is simply not an option. Every day spent not drawing is another wasted opportunity that's never coming back. I just want to get something down - regardless of it's quality. What's important is I can put down some physical evidence of the worlds that plague my mind. If I'm not having fun working on something I finish it as quickly as possible to move on to the next thing.

My Muses

I make no exaggeration when I say I would of killed myself if it weren't for my OCs. If you ever been unfortunate enough to meet me in real life, I am very likely to be staring blanky into the distance - daydreaming. I find it difficult to experience life as it is and need to disassociate to let the little guys in my head relive my present. They are the subject of the majority of my art and you really ought to get familiar with them!
