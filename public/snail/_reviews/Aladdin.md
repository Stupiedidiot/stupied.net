The CGI is really fun and the color composition for the night scenes are so beautiful. Love how fun and exaggerated the caricatures are. Especially enjoyed how intricate Jafar's movements are. Aside from that, this probably the ugliest film in the Disney renaissance ear.

Lowkey Princess Jasmine might be my least favourite Disney Princess purely because of how selfish she is. Like, marrying for love is awesome and all but like, your a *princess*???? Who will be responsible for the welfare of her people? Girl doesn't even understand how you're supposed to *pay* for stuff at the market?
