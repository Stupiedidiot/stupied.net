This film's tone is so... strange. It starts off with a really wholesome segment with Mr. Strok and all. Then theres is some genuinely depressing scenes like Dumbo's mother getting reprimanded by the ringmaster and Dumbo's visit to her cell. Then the film slaps you with [Pink Elephants](https://youtu.be/jcZUPDMXzJ8). Don't get me wrong, I absolutely adore how out of pocket it is but like??? How am I even supposed to feel about this film when it brushes so many horrifying implications aside. Also ["When I See an Elephant Fly"](https://youtu.be/_v2exWrsGOc) is such a feel good tune, I'm not even mad at the crows ripping on Dumbo haha.

Anyways, on a side tangent, I really enjoy Dumbo being non-verbal (very bias opinion). I like how well the animator utilized his ears for showing his emotions.  Things like twitching his ears in happiness or hiding behind them in shame. Timothy is also a really charming character. *Insert envisioning the man I want to be meme here*.
