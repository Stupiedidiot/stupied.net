An honourable first attempt at a fully colored short, but it's definitely rough. You can still see the animators cling into harsh black shading which, when put against a colourful environment, just looks really sloppy lineart. They haven't quite figured out what they wanted to do with the shading and it keeps changing from shot to shot. One moment they have a highlights and awkward purple pillow shading, then the next shot they just gave up and left them unshaded. Aside from that, I'm a big fan of all the characterization. Like how old man tree having a lizard for a tongue. Also really love the pine tree characterized as a bird with little chicks and the flame with tiny legs. So cute. It's fun to look back at this short and seeing how much Disney improve their technique the years after.
