This sequel lacks a lot of the charm of the original movie. Especially disliked how bastardized the duo's personalities are. Max was a massive jerk to Goofy in the previous movie too but at least it was grounded in fairly realistic insecurities. He's a teenager in high school! A time in your life where you're hyper-vigilant of how others perceive you and are consumed by an overwhelming need to fit in. In the original film you can actually see Max putting aside his fears and making a genuine effort to bond with Goofy. Seriously, what excuse does college Max have? He tells Goofy to piss off so he can laze around in a hippie coffee shop and skateboard all day?

You could make an argument that Max's issue is Goofy's clinginess and the point of the film was letting him finally grow up. But then what was the point of the whole "Daadd I need you :(" segment. The themes of this film is so disjointed and half realized, which would be whatever if I didn't hold  the previous film at such a high regard. Also for a movie that supposed to be about accepting things passing by, it fails to show any restrain for the final scene. So Max helps out the bad guy *and* wins the race? Like wow, how convenient. I think it would made for a far more effective ending if Max didn't win the race. Not that it would make this movie any less of a self-indulgent nostalgia bait. Oh well, at least the dance numbers were fun and the women were hot.
