The more I look into older Duck media, the more I understand Don Rosa's dislike for the Ducktales series. Unfortunately, this was the catalyst for my obsession of these lil guys so my opinion will be incredibly biased. Aside from the wet-dogification of Donald, I really enjoy the reimagined personalities. I love how the triplets have their own distinct personalities outside of being more competent than Donald. I love how Webby is full of love and excitement but is kinda weird and intense because of it. I love how Della Duck is finally a fully realized character. She a soccer mom but it's okay I forgive her. I love its nods to the comics and the inclusion of "obscure" characters. Not to fond of how they messed with Matilda but that's besides the point. I love how Scrooge's descent to monetary greed was a coping system for the grief he felt. I love literally ever single villain (yes, even you Mark Beaks) and all the side characters in the filler episode. Like Lin Manuel canonically being in the Ducktales canon is so funny to me you guys have no idea. Also the women, like hello??? Daisy Duck, Magica De Spell, and Goldie!!!!! Can you spell iconic? It strikes such a wonderful balance between modernization and paying tribute its source. It set out to introduce these almost a century year old characters to a newer generation and, based on my zoomer ass gushing over it, it has done its job well.
