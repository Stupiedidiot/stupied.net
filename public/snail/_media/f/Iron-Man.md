A solid foundation to the MCU! The movie is pretty fast paced but not in an overstimulating way. You basically learn everything you need to know about Stark within the first few minuets. Based on how quickly he responds to the reporter interrogating him, you can tell how much he believes in the good of what he does. Then the kidnapping happens. He witnesses the abuse first hand and begins rethinking his entire philosophy. In concept, Tony Stark should be an insufferable character; Egotistical, impulsive, misogynistic,  multimillionaire profiting out of his pro-war propaganda. Yet, because of how well Robert Downey Jr. plays him out, you can't help but be charmed. Underneath all those vices is a person who genuinely wants to see the world become a better place. I enjoy how the stakes aren't other worldly. Just some guy bitter of how frivolously Stark treats the company. Also I love Pepper soooooo much. You guys have no idea. Anyways, all around a fun film. This is probably my favorite Marvel film of all time...!
