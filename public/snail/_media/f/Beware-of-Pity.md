Lowkey didn't enjoy reading this book. A lot of Hofmiller inner monologue is just him justifying his actions by regurgitating the same arguments. This book is basically what it's like being a bippie's favorite person. The constant back in forth from showering of love to spiteful distrust. I really *really* dislike Edith. Like, yes, her circumstance is horrible but that doesn't give her the right to suicide bait someone into loving her. She's just a spoiled rich girl that has never develop the mental fortitude to accept no as an answer. It's probably why becoming lame is so difficult for her. It's because she can't guilt trip her way out of it. She has to come to terms with her past carelessness since no amount of money and coddling will ever gift her bodily anatomy back. The only think I enjoyed was the introduction and the second story of this novel. The later was really cute. Wished most of the melodrama was cut up. There is no need for this book to be this long bruh.
