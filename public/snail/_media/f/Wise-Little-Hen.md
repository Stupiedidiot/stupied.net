The short that marks the animated debut of Donald Duck! Amazing how even in his first appearance, he's still a little piece of shit. You'd never had guess he'd become the icon he would be today. Honestly, I love how narrow and dorky looking his original design was. The animal noises paired with the lines were really cute. Peter Pig and Donald Duck's relationship is really funny and I wish there was more of their shenanigans.
