I have a pretty high tolerance for distasteful racial stereotypes in old cartoons but man, this one is just *so* bad. It isn't like one off gags or irrelevant background characters. They literally take up half of the screentime. It's honestly such a shame because the concept of toys fixing each other is really cute. Many of the Silly Symphonies shorts is filled with a pro-war messaging. So this short being motivating without the existence of an enemy to defeat through violence is a really nice change of pace. I love how rigid but intimate the movements are. Each toy is carefully constructed with joints accounted for and seams delicately drawn in. But seeing how frequently the two black caricatures are used as comic relief undermines any sort of whimsy it has. There is no reason for a short about uplifting one another to put down an already marginalized group.
