This is novella is my introduction Stefan Zweig and I've enjoyed it quite thoroughly. It's really interesting to witness Dr. B slow descent into madness because it isn't through gruesome elaborate torture methods. Instead we experience the aching pain of nothingness. Having that itch but not owning the arms to reach it. Isolation, something I've long glorified, is put into a grim light. Particularly liked the writing style of the book. You never really get to read the narrators dialogue which leaves rooms for quirkiness of the other characters. A short but entertaining read.
