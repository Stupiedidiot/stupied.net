About

I absolutely adore the concept of habit trackers but always struggled with maintaining them one. I started off with highlighting the days a paper print out calendar but kept forgetting what each color colerated to. Most digital habit trackers are far more rigid and the ui are filled with prompts asking you to pay premium. Which is why this page exists! Also, because of how many hobbies I have, it be unrealistic to force myself to do each one everyday. So instead each habit is catergorized into more broader topics!
