My fixation on Disney media begun when I re-watched Ducktales (2017) around October of 2025. It got me interested in looking into older Donald Duck shorts and comics. This in turn led to a fascination of the company's overall history. It got me realizing that the amount of Disney media I've already seen was just a small fraction of the all the art the company has made. So it's fun to look back on films I adored as a kid and learning about ones that I've overlooked. If you couldn't tell, I have a strong preference for earnest story telling, cartoony 2D art styles, and over the top musical numbers.
