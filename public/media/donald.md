My fixation on this stupid ass duck started around October of 2025 when I decided to rewatch Ducktales (2017). Since then, it has been my mission to watch all this 90 year old man's failures. Woah boy, there is a lot. Truthfully, I'm not quite sure what is it about Donald that interest me. Maybe it's because he's primary composed of my favorite colors. Maybe it's because I find him facing the consequences of his actions really funny. Maybe because I admire his sense of adventure and love for his family. Dunno.
